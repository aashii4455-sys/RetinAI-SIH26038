🏥 Rural Screening Workflow

A simple digital pathway for preliminary screening and referral support

📷
1. Capture Obtain a retinal fundus image using an available imaging device.
🤖
2. AI Screen The model classifies the image across five DR severity grades.
🧠
3. Explain Present the predicted grade, confidence and Grad-CAM visual explanation.
🩺
4. Refer Support appropriate follow-up with a qualified eye-care professional.
RetinAI is an AI-assisted screening prototype. Results are not a medical diagnosis and should not replace examination by a qualified eye-care professional.
